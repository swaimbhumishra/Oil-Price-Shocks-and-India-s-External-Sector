# Oil-Price-Shocks-and-India-s-External-Sector
Independent policy research paper examining the macroeconomic impact of oil price shocks on India’s external sector, exchange rate dynamics, inflation, and monetary policy transmission.

## Implications for Exchange Rate Dynamics and Monetary Policy

This repository contains an independent policy research paper examining the macroeconomic transmission of oil price shocks onto India's external sector, with a focus on exchange rate dynamics, imported inflation, current account pressures, and monetary policy response under the Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) framework.

The paper analyses how geopolitically driven disruptions in global crude oil markets affect India's macroeconomic stability, particularly through external sector vulnerability, forex market pressures, inflation transmission mechanisms, and RBI policy trade-offs.

---

## Key Areas Covered

- India's structural crude oil import dependence
- Current Account Deficit (CAD) and external vulnerability
- Exchange rate dynamics and RBI intervention
- Imported inflation and cost-push transmission
- Monetary policy response under the FIT framework
- Geopolitical supply shocks and energy security
- Long-term implications of energy transition and EV adoption
